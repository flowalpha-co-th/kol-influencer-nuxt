# Sleek Design Brief — Ripples Home Page (Mobile, 4 Variants)

## 1. Project Context

**Ripples** is Thailand's #1 KOL marketplace — a mobile-first web app that connects Thai content creators with verified brands. This is a **creator-facing** home page: every word, photo, and CTA should speak directly to creators (influencers, micro-influencers, content makers) who want to earn money by partnering with brands they trust.

We are NOT designing for brands here. The user is a 22–35 year old Thai creator on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube who has an audience but wants steady, fair-paying brand deals without the chaos of DM negotiations, fake-engagement competitors, and tax paperwork.

The product was recently rebranded from "Prakast" to **Ripples** — the name reflects how a single creator's voice ripples outward to their audience and to brands. This metaphor is **central to the visual identity** and must appear in every variant.

---

## 2. Deliverable

Generate **4 high-fidelity mobile mockups** of the Ripples home page, each in a distinct visual style.

- **Viewport:** 390 × ~2800px (full vertical scroll, iPhone 14 base)
- **All 4 variants must cover the same 8 sections** (listed below) — only the visual treatment changes
- **Output:** Figma-ready, layered, exportable at @2x
- **Each variant is a complete, scrollable page** — not just hero variations

---

## 3. Brand Lockup (non-negotiable across all 4 variants)

### Logo
The Ripples wordmark uses navy letters with **yellow concentric ripple arcs forming the loops of the "p" letters**. The ripple arcs are the heart of the brand — they expand outward like sound waves or a stone hitting water. Use the logo as-is at the top of every variant. Do not redesign or recolor it.

### Color Palette (strict — these are the ONLY colors)
- **Navy** `#516171` — primary text, primary surfaces, dark backgrounds
- **Yellow** `#FEE601` — CTAs, accents, ripple glows, highlight backgrounds
- **Off-white** `#F8F9FA` — light backgrounds, card surfaces
- **Pure white** `#FFFFFF` — text on dark, button text on navy

**FORBIDDEN:** orange, red, purple, green, pink, gradients drifting toward warm tones. Even in the dark variant, glows stay yellow.

### Typography
- **Font:** Kanit (Google Fonts) — supports Thai + Latin
- **Weights:** 300 (light body), 400 (body), 500 (subheads), 600 (headings), 700 (display)
- **Line heights:** 1.05 (display), 1.2 (heading), 1.6 (body)

### Ripple Motif (must appear in every variant)
Concentric circle rings (3 stacked, decreasing opacity outward) are the brand's decorative DNA. They float as background accents, frame photos, define glow halos, or scatter as subtle pattern. Every variant uses them differently — but every variant uses them.

### Photography Rules
- Natural daylight, neutral color grading
- Real Thai creators in real settings (bedroom desk with ring light, café shooting reels, friend filming on the street)
- **NO duotone, NO heavy filters, NO oversaturated edits** — yellow comes from UI overlays only, never from the photo itself
- Diverse representation across gender, age (22–35), and creator vertical (beauty, food, tech, fashion, gaming, lifestyle, travel)

---

## 4. Audience & Tone

- **Primary user:** Thai creator, 22–35, mobile-first, scrolls in Thai but understands UI in English
- **Tone:** Warm, credible, peer-to-peer. Like a senior creator friend giving career advice — not corporate, not bro-marketing
- **Avoid:** "10x your followers", "go viral", "hustle culture", influencer-clichés, fake-luxury imagery
- **Embrace:** "Get paid on time", "Real engagement, real brands", "Your work, valued properly"
- **UI language:** Default Thai with EN toggle. All copy below provided in both languages.

## 6. The 4 Style Variants

All 4 cover the same 8 sections above. Only the visual treatment changes.

### VARIANT 1 — "Lifestyle Authentic"
Inspired by the bright/minimal cycling-club layout.

- **Mood:** Real, warm, human, energetic. Feels like flipping through a Thai lifestyle magazine.
- **Background:** White and off-white `#F8F9FA`, broken up by bold solid yellow `#FEE601` content blocks
- **Hero:** Right ~55% is a full-bleed lifestyle photo of a Thai creator (woman, mid-20s, holding phone, smiling, natural daylight, ring light blurred behind her). Left ~45% is white with the bold display headline. Black pill chips ("Beauty", "Lifestyle", "Authentic") float on top of the photo at jaunty angles.
- **About section:** Single big yellow block, navy heading, navy body — same rhythm as the cycling ref's "This Is Who We Are" block
- **Featured Campaigns:** Asymmetric — one dark navy card on left, one bright yellow card on right, with oversized stats inside ("72K reach", "93 campaigns live", "10K+ creators")
- **Buttons:** Navy `#516171` rounded pill with yellow `#FEE601` arrow icon top-right corner
- **Ripple motif:** Thin outline-stroke ripples scattered in the margins, low-opacity, decorative

### VARIANT 2 — "Friendly Studio"
Inspired by the BrandBuzz light/illustrated layout.

- **Mood:** Approachable, helpful, tutorial-feeling. Feels like a friendly onboarding app.
- **Background:** Soft cream `#FFFBE0` with crisp white cards. Pale yellow polka-dot scatter in corners.
- **Hero:** Custom 3D-style isometric illustration of a creator avatar with floating UI elements orbiting them — phone showing a dashboard, a paycheck icon, a verified badge, a small concentric-ripple ring. Headline sits to the side. Illustration style: rounded, soft shadows, navy + yellow only.
- **Services section:** 6 rounded white cards in 2-col grid, each with a colorful flat icon (still navy + yellow only), centered title, 1-line description, "Read more" arrow.
- **How It Works:** Numbered list with large circular navy badges (1, 2, 3, 4), illustrated character interacting with each step on the right.
- **Testimonials:** Carousel of white cards with creator avatars, star ratings, quote, name.
- **Buttons:** Yellow `#FEE601` fully rounded pill with navy text. Soft drop shadow.
- **Ripple motif:** Filled concentric rings as illustration accents, polka-dot scatter

### VARIANT 3 — "Aspirational Glow"
Inspired by the Studio Pley dark/dreamy layout.

- **Mood:** Premium, dreamy, ambitious. "Next-level creator energy."
- **Background:** Dark navy gradient `#516171 → #2D3742 → #1A2028`, with yellow neon ripple glows pulsing behind the hero.
- **Hero:** Cinematic 3D rendered scene — a phone floats centered, glowing yellow concentric ripple rings expand outward from it like a sound wave, soft particle dust drifts upward, a creator silhouette stands in the deep background. Headline sits at the top, primary CTA below the phone. Heavy use of glow, bokeh, atmosphere.
- **Stats Strip:** Three glowing yellow numerals on dark backdrop, each with a faint ripple halo
- **Services section:** 3-column dark cards with yellow glowing top border, small flat icon, title, description. Hover state = stronger glow.
- **How It Works:** Vertical timeline with dotted yellow connector line, each step as a glass-morphism card on the dark backdrop
- **Featured Campaigns:** Dark cards with glowing yellow accent edges, campaign image, glowing budget pill
- **Testimonials:** One big hero quote card centered, soft yellow halo behind it
- **Buttons:** Yellow `#FEE601` rounded pill with soft outer glow (yellow drop shadow blur 24px, opacity 40%)
- **Ripple motif:** This variant IS the motif. Concentric rings are the dominant visual everywhere — hero, section dividers, button glows, background pattern.

### VARIANT 4 — "Pro Dashboard"
Inspired by the BizNext dark/corporate/photorealistic layout.

- **Mood:** Professional, growth-focused, "I'm serious about my creator career". The vibe of a fintech SaaS for creators.
- **Background:** Deep navy `#516171` gradient to slate, with bright yellow accent line patterns (like dashboard chart grids) at low opacity.
- **Hero:** Photorealistic 3D render of a creator's desk — a sleek laptop showing the Ripples Earnings Dashboard mockup (yellow line chart trending up + earnings number "฿128,500"), a phone next to it showing a campaign approval screen, soft window light from the side, a coffee cup, a notebook with the Ripples logo embossed. Headline floats above with a thin yellow underline accent.
- **Trust strip below hero:** Logo row of fake brand marks ("Trusted by 500+ brands like:") in muted white on dark
- **Services section:** 4-tile horizontal feature row at the top ("Browse", "Apply", "Track", "Get Paid") with glowing flat icons + 1-line desc, then 6-card service grid below in 2-col, each card dark with yellow icon + hover glow
- **Stats Strip:** Oversized horizontal stat bar — "10,000+ creators · 98% on-time payment · 500+ verified brands · 24/7 support" separated by glowing yellow dots
- **How It Works:** 4 numbered steps as horizontal cards with progress indicator
- **Featured Campaigns:** Dark dashboard-style cards with mini analytics preview, glowing chart lines, "Apply" button
- **Buttons:** Yellow `#FEE601` outline rounded pill with arrow icon (primary) + ghost white "Watch Demo" with play icon (secondary). Subtle glow on hover.
- **Ripple motif:** Background pattern at 5% opacity behind hero, glow accents on chart axes, ripple as a stat divider

## 7. Non-Negotiables (checklist Sleek must hit)

- [ ] Navy `#516171` and Yellow `#FEE601` are the ONLY accent colors in every variant
- [ ] White / off-white / dark navy gradients are the ONLY background colors
- [ ] Ripple/concentric-circle motif appears in every variant (composition, glow, pattern, or accent)
- [ ] Logo placed at top-left of every variant, unmodified
- [ ] Kanit font (or Kanit-equivalent if Sleek substitutes)
- [ ] All copy creator-facing (never "find creators", always "find campaigns")
- [ ] Bilingual copy provided where shown (TH primary, EN alongside)
- [ ] Photography: natural Thai creators, no duotone, no over-edited filters
- [ ] Mobile viewport 390×~2800px, all 8 sections visible on scroll

---

## 8. What to Avoid

- Orange, purple, green, red — even as gradient drift toward warm tones
- AI-generic stock illustrations of "diverse people in suits" or generic cartoon influencers
- Bro-marketing energy ("10x your followers", "the secret to going viral", "hustle harder")
- Fake luxury imagery (champagne, sports cars, beach mansions)
- Heavy filters / duotones / oversaturated photography
- Sans-serif fonts that aren't Kanit-compatible (Thai needs to render)
- Brand-facing copy ("Find the right creator" — flip everything to creator POV)
- Crypto / web3 aesthetics
- Cluttered hero — keep breathing room

---

## Final Note to Sleek

Treat the 4 variants as 4 different agencies pitching the same product. Each must feel internally consistent and complete — not 4 minor color-swaps of the same layout. The throughline is Ripples: navy + yellow, ripple motif, Thai creator audience, creator-facing voice. Everything else can flex.
